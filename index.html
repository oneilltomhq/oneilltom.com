Everything moving is live — a Hydra video synth compiled to WGSL via Three.js TSL, running on WebGPU in this tab right now. Because each patch compiles to a node graph rather than a fullscreen shader, the outputs are just materials. The liquid is a raymarched smooth-min SDF — no geometry, just the fragment shader — wearing the previous frame of the scene as a warped mirror. Three invisible spinning gravity wells with different temperaments trade the globules between them; click anywhere to splash. The physics is hand-rolled, no engine.
