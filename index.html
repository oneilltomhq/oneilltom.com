Everything moving is live — a Hydra video synth compiled to WGSL via Three.js TSL, running on WebGPU in this tab right now. Because each patch compiles to a node graph rather than a fullscreen shader, the outputs are just materials — a swarm of 128 bodies wears them, mixed per face in the shader. The boxes are live mirrors: every face re-projects the previous frame of the whole scene, recursion until the pixels run out.
